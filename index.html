Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive the guide?

Right after checkout, you'll receive an email with your download link for the guide (PDF). You can also download it from your order confirmation page.

What is included with WealthMe?

WealthMe is a personal finance web app that helps you organize your income, expenses, debts and investments in one place, with a breakdown of your spending by category and 12-month insights. It runs in your browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store.

When will I receive app access?

The guide is yours right after checkout. As an Early Bird, you'll receive your WealthMe license key by email the moment the app launches, with lifetime access.

Where is my financial data stored?

Only on your own device. WealthMe doesn't send your information to any server, so we never see your financial data. Because of that, your data doesn't sync between devices: if you activate your license key on another device, the app will open there empty, without the information from your first device.

What is the refund policy?

Because the Financial Security Guide and Financial Security System are digital products, all sales are final and we don't offer refunds for change of mind. You can read the full refund policy here.
